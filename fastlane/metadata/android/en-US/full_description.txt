LightCraft is a photo editor and raw developer for Android, built on the open-source LightCraft engine (written in Rust) with a Kotlin / Jetpack Compose interface. It follows the layout and the tools of Lightroom mobile, and everything stays on your phone: no account, no cloud, no internet access.

Edit
* Light: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, and a point or parametric tone curve (also drawn right on the photo)
* Color: white balance with an eyedropper, temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation, an 8-band colour mixer and colour grading wheels
* Effects, Detail and Optics: texture, clarity, dehaze, vignette, grain, sharpening, noise reduction, lens corrections
* Profiles and presets, with an amount; copy and paste settings between photos

Masking
* Brush, linear and radial gradients, colour range and luminance range (picked from a point or an area)
* Add to and subtract from masks; each part has its own menu (hide, rename, duplicate, convert, delete)

Retouch and geometry
* Remove, heal and clone spots
* Crop with ratios and social formats, straighten, Upright, rotate and flip, manual transforms

Library
* Albums, filters, sorting, search, select mode, grids and a slideshow
* Import from the photo picker, files (raw formats included), the camera or Share from other apps

Save and share
* JPG, TIF, AVIF, DNG for raws, or the original; full or custom size, quality, sharpening, colour space, metadata, watermark (text or graphic) and border

Works upright and sideways. LightCraft is in beta.
